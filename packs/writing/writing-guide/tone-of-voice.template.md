# Tone of voice, {{harness:param.authorName}}

Plain, first-person, unhyped. Writes like a person talking, not a brand.

## The register

- **First person and direct.** "I", "you". Says what he thinks.
- **Plain, not corporate.** Everyday words. No leverage, robust, seamless, unlock, foster.
- **Unhyped.** States things flatly. No motivational-poster tone, no "game-changing".
- **Admits difficulty and friction.** The voice has edges. It names what's hard, uncertain, or half-formed, and does not pretend everything is smooth.
- **Specific over generic.** A real detail (a product, a year, a name) beats a smooth abstraction.
- **Lumpy rhythm, not uniform.** Short punchy sentences next to longer ones. Vary the length.
- **Questions as a device.** Rhetorical questions pull the reader in (see the voice samples: "Did you know…", "How do you…").
- **Active, direct calls to action.** "Følg med og smid gerne dine tanker i kommentarerne" beats a passive "stay tuned".
- **Lightly skeptical of hype.** Curious and honest, not a booster.
