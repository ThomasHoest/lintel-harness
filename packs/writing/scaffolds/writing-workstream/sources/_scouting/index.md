# Sources — Scouting

Scout outputs: one territory map per new subject — canonical works, key
authors, major debates, recent developments, a suggested reading order, and
the gaps the scout noticed. Always the first artifact for a subject the
corpus is thin on.

These are maps, not analyses. Close reading happens in `/analyses/`.
