# Example workstream — Drafts

Drafts for this workstream. See the [workstream hub](../index.md).

Versioned filenames, never overwrites: `<piece>-v1.md`, `<piece>-v1.1.md`.
Superseded drafts move to `archive/` rather than being deleted. The `writer`
drafts against an approved outline; the `editor` edits in place after the critic
loop closes.
