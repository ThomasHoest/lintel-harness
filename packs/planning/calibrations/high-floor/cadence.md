# Review cadence — defaults at a high constraint floor

**Calibrated content.** This file came from the `high-floor` calibration.
The other pole is readable at
`.harness/pack/calibrations/near-zero-floor/cadence.md`.

> **Cadence follows uncertainty resolution, not the calendar.** That rule
> does not vary. What varies is how fast uncertainty resolves here — and
> at a high constraint floor, it resolves **slowly**.

---

## Defaults

| Trigger | Default here |
|---|---|
| **Primary trigger** | A gated event: a submission, a review outcome, a test-article result, a supply or tooling milestone, a regulatory response |
| **Committed-milestone review** | At each programme gate, and on any result that moves a determinant |
| **Portfolio intake pass** | Two to four times a year — the register does not turn over faster than the programmes in it |
| **Calendar backstop** | Six months. No bet goes longer than this unreviewed, whatever the evidence is doing |
| **Kill-criteria escalation** | Immediate, always. This is not a cadence setting |

## Why the defaults are slow here

Uncertainty at a high constraint floor resolves at the speed of an
external process — a clearance cycle, a trial read-out, a certification
review, a supply lead time. Reviewing more often than the evidence
arrives does not produce better decisions; it produces the *appearance*
of governance while consuming review capacity the absorption gate needs.

The failure mode this calibration guards against is the **ritual
review**: a quarterly meeting that fires on a bet whose determinant has
not moved since the last one, and produces a status update rather than a
decision.

## Where the calendar still earns its place

The backstop is not decoration. A programme with an 18-to-36 month shape
can go a long time with nothing visibly happening, and "nothing has
moved" is a claim that needs checking rather than assuming. At six months
the check runs whether or not a determinant moved — and finding that
nothing moved, on the record, is a legitimate result.

## What does not vary with this file

- The gate is **non-delegable**, at every calibration.
- A crossed kill criterion escalates **immediately**, never at the next
  scheduled review.
- The loop closes: a review with no intake consequence has not finished.
